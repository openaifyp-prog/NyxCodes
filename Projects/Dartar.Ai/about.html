Driven by Experience

Darter was built by industry veterans who understand the challenges of modern logistics. We're here to change the game.

Our Story

Before Darter was a software company, we were dispatchers, drivers, and fleet owners. We spent years navigating the complexities of the transportation industry, dealing with outdated tools, inefficient routes, and constant communication breakdowns.

We knew there had to be a better way. So, we decided to build it ourselves.

Founded in New York, Darter.ai combines deep industry knowledge with cutting-edge technology. Our mission is simple: to empower fleet operators with the tools they need to run safer, more efficient, and more profitable businesses.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide everything we do.

Safety First

We prioritize the safety of drivers and cargo above all else. Our technology is designed to reduce risks and promote safe driving habits.

Efficiency

We believe in doing more with less. Our algorithms squeeze every ounce of efficiency out of your operations to maximize your bottom line.

Transparency

No hidden fees, no black boxes. We believe in open communication and clear data.
