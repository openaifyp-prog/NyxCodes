The Stone House

Reykjavik, Iceland

Overview

  • Year: 2024
  • Area: 450 m²
  • Client: Private
  • Status: Completed

Nestled in the rugged Icelandic landscape, The Stone House is a dialogue between brutalism and nature. The concrete structure mimics the surrounding basalt rocks, while expansive glazing frames the Northern Lights. The interior features locally sourced stone, warm timber paneling, and a minimalist aesthetic that allows the environment to take center stage.
