About Us

Driving sustainable change through data and community.

Our Mission

Empowering Africa to Manage Waste Smarter

Clean Africa was founded with a singular purpose: to revolutionize waste management across the continent. We believe that lack of data shouldn't be the barrier to a cleaner environment. By leveraging cutting-edge technology and fostering strong community partnerships, we are building a future where waste is seen not as a problem, but as a resource.

Our platform connects municipalities, recyclers, and citizens, creating a transparent ecosystem that promotes accountability and efficiency.
