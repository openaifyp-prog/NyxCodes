Our Solutions

Innovative technologies for a cleaner tomorrow.

GIS Waste Mapping

Visualizing waste hotspots with high-precision satellite data to optimize collection routes.

Predictive Analytics

Forecasting waste generation trends to help municipalities plan resources efficiently.

Consulting Services

Expert policy advice for governments and private sectors to implement sustainable standards.

Building Smart Cities

Our integration doesn't stop at waste collection. We work with city planners to integrate IoT (Internet of Things) sensors into public bins, providing real-time fill-level data. This enables dynamic collection schedules, reducing fuel consumption and traffic congestion.

  • Reduced operational costs by up to 30%
  • Cleaner streets and parks
  • Lower carbon emissions from collection trucks
