Why Clean Africa?

We bridge the gap between waste generation and sustainable management. By providing real-time data and actionable insights, we empower municipalities, governments, and organizations to make informed decisions that lead to cleaner cities and a healthier environment.

Join Us

WHAT CAN YOU DO WITH CLEAN AFRICA?

For Municipalities

Gain full visibility into waste collection efficiency and landfill statuses. Optimize routes, reduce operational costs, and improve service delivery to citizens with data-backed strategies.

Researchers

Access comprehensive datasets on waste generation patterns, composition, and disposal methods. Support your environmental studies with accurate, ground-truth data from across the continent.

Government

Formulate effective waste management policies and monitor compliance. Our platform provides the oversight needed to enforce regulations and track national environmental goals.

International Organisations

Collaborate on large-scale sanitation projects with reliable data. Monitor the impact of funding and interventions to ensure sustainable development objectives are met.
